# Fan & battery pack: visual spec (from user-supplied product photos)

Sources: `assets_raw/fan_reference/*.png` (product listing photos, ~500px; reference only, not for on-screen use).

## Blower (fan)
- Type: small **centrifugal "snail-shell" blower** (volute housing), NOT an axial computer fan.
- Size: ~10 cm tall.
- Color: bright orange plastic. Base `#F7700A`, shade `#E35C11`, deep shade `#C9400C`, highlight `#FF9A3C`.
- Front (intake) face: round opening with a **radial spoke grille** (~16 spokes) and a small hub.
- Rim: scalloped, ribbed edge around the housing.
- Outlet: rectangular nozzle extending from the side, with **horizontal slots/vents**.
- Back: domed motor cover with small screw points; mounting tabs on the rim.
- Cable: thin black cable ending in a **USB-A male plug**.

## Battery pack
- Orange rectangular box, ~9 × 7 cm, rounded corners, same orange family as the blower.
- Holds **4 × AA** (shown in the listing photo); lid on the front.
- **USB-A female port** on the top edge (the blower's USB plugs into it).
- Back: vertical **belt clip**.
- Small slide switch on the box (front lower edge in the angle photo).

## Two ways to run it (listing photo)
1. Blower USB → battery pack (4 × AA).
2. Blower USB → portable power bank (only on models that support it, per pumpit.co.il FAQ).

## Consequences for production
- `03_TUTORIAL_REFERENCE/01_Installing_Fan_AA_Battery_Reference_AI.png` shows a black axial fan and a black open AA holder: **wrong hardware. DO NOT USE.**
- All fan and battery close-ups are built as accurate vector or 2.5D illustrations in Remotion from this spec.
- Kling shots must not feature the fan in close-up. If the fan appears and is distorted, the shot is replaced.

## Placement on the costume (user-supplied reference: `fan_placement_trex_exterior.png`)
- On the full inflatable T-Rex, the blower's **round intake grille faces outward**. It sits on the **lower back/side of the body, just above the hip and tail base**.
- From outside you see only the round orange-red spoke grille, flush with the fabric. The housing is inside the costume.
- The listing uses a **circular magnifier callout** (zoom bubble with a pointer line) to show it. We reuse this device in Scene 02.
